HOW TO PLAY

4 Simple Steps to Play & Earn Cash
STEP 1
⚔️

Choose & Join a Battle

Pick your favorite game mode (Ludo Classic, Quick, or Snakes) and select a challenge from the Open Battles Lobby with your desired entry fee.

STEP 2
🔑

Copy Ludo King Room Code

Once matched, tap "COPY ROOM CODE" in the Match Room. Then click "OPEN LUDO KING" to launch your game app instantly.

STEP 3
🎮

Play with Opponent in Ludo King

In Ludo King, go to Play with Friends › Join, paste the code, and play the match. Remember to take a screenshot of the victory screen when you win!

STEP 4
🏆

Upload Screenshot & Claim Cash

Return to the Match Result screen, select "I WON", attach your victory screenshot proof, and submit. The prize money will be credited directly to your wallet!
