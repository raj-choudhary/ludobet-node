GAME RULES

Official Tournament Standards & Policies
🎲 Ludo Classic Rules (1v1 & 4P)
  • Opening Token: Rolling a 6 or 1 is required to open a token from the base.
  • Winning Condition: The first player to bring all 4 tokens home wins the prize pool.
  • Extra Rolls: You get an extra dice roll on rolling a 6 or when you cut an opponent's token.
  • Three 6s Penalty: Rolling three consecutive 6s skips your turn automatically.
  • Safe Zones: Star cells and starting home colored squares are 100% safe from cuts.
⚡ Ludo Quick / 1-Token Rules
  • 1-Token Victory: Bring just 1 token home (after cutting at least 1 opponent token) to win immediately!
  • Time Limit: Fast 2-minute sprint format with 15-second per turn timer.
  • Mandatory Cut: You must eliminate at least one opponent pawn before entering the home victory path.
🐍 Snakes & Ladders (1-100 Rush)
  • Race to 100: First player to land on tile 100 with exact dice roll wins.
  • Ladders: Instantly climb from the ladder base to its top.
  • Snakes: Sliding down from the snake head to its tail.
  • Extra Roll on 6: Rolling a 6 earns one bonus throw.
⚖️ Fairplay & Anti-Fraud Policy
  • Screenshot Proof: Winner must upload a clean, unedited screenshot of the victory screen within 15 minutes.
  • Disconnection: If a player disconnects, they forfeit the match and entry fee.
  • Penalty for Fake Results: Submitting fake or edited winning proof will result in an instant ₹100 penalty + permanent account ban.
  • Zero Tolerance on Cheating: Using modded APKs or bots is strictly prohibited.
