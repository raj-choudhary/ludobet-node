How To Play & Win

Climb ladders to jump ahead and avoid deadly snake bites!

1
🪜 Ladder Boosts (Fast Advance)

2
🐍 Snake Bites (Slide Down)
Landing on a snake's mouth slides you down to its tail. In 24-Move Rush, you lose score points!
3
🏁 Winning the Match
Race Mode: First player to reach square 100 wins! Rush Mode: Highest points at move 24 wins!
